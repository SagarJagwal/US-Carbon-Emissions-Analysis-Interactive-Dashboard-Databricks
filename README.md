# US Carbon Emissions Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Databricks dashboard exploring United States emissions
data collected by the **U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) in
2023**. The dashboard presents emissions patterns across states and
counties and examines how emissions per person relate to population.

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard contains four main visualizations:

1.  **Emissions Across the Continental United States** -- A geographic
    view of emissions distribution.
2.  **Emissions vs. Population** -- A scatter plot comparing emissions
    per person with population.
3.  **Top 10 States by Emissions Contribution** -- A chart showing the
    percentage contribution of the leading states.
4.  **County-Level CO₂ Emissions** -- A ranking of counties by total
    emissions, measured in million tons of CO₂.

## Objectives

-   Explore the geographic distribution of emissions across the United
    States.
-   Compare emissions across states and counties.
-   Examine the relationship between population and per-person
    emissions.
-   Identify major contributors to the reported emissions.
-   Present the findings in an interactive, easy-to-understand
    dashboard.
